LESSON: Alameda / CA Food Handler — Contamination Control
LANGUAGE: en
JURISDICTION: us-ca-alameda
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
SUMMARY: Cross-contamination, sanitizing, pests, and inspection readiness. Prep only.

SLIDE 1 | Cross-contamination
Keep raw proteins separate from ready-to-eat foods. Use separate boards and utensils or wash-rinse-sanitize between uses. Never let raw juices drip onto other foods.
NARRATION: Keep raw and ready-to-eat foods completely separate.

SLIDE 2 | Clean then sanitize
Scrape, wash with detergent, rinse, sanitize at correct concentration, then air dry. Sanitizing dirty surfaces does not work.
NARRATION: Clean first, then sanitize — never skip the wash step.

SLIDE 3 | Sanitizer strength
Mix chlorine, quat, or iodine per label. Verify with test strips and replace dirty or weak solutions.
NARRATION: Test sanitizer strength with strips before each use.

SLIDE 4 | FIFO stock rotation
First In, First Out: place new stock behind older product and use the oldest first to reduce spoilage risk.
NARRATION: Use older stock first — FIFO every time.

SLIDE 5 | Pest prevention
Seal gaps, store food sealed, clean spills, and remove clutter. Use licensed pest control — do not spray pesticides over food areas yourself.
NARRATION: Deny pests food, water, and shelter, and call licensed pest control.

SLIDE 6 | Allergen cross-contact
Allergens need dedicated handling: clean equipment, separate prep, and accurate menu communication. Cleaning alone may not remove allergen residues.
NARRATION: Treat allergen requests as critical every time.

SLIDE 7 | Common pathogens
Know high-risk associations: Salmonella with poultry and eggs, STEC with ground beef and produce, Listeria with deli and ready-to-eat foods, norovirus with hands and ready-to-eat foods.
NARRATION: Hygiene and correct cooking temperatures stop the most common pathogens.

SLIDE 8 | Health inspections
Alameda County Environmental Health inspects for critical violations that can cause illness. Keep logs, fix problems immediately, and ask supervisors when unsure.
NARRATION: Critical violations get fixed immediately — ask if you are unsure.

SLIDE 9 | If a guest gets sick
Take complaints seriously, notify a manager, preserve suspected food if instructed, and cooperate with investigators.
NARRATION: Report foodborne illness complaints to a manager right away.

SLIDE 10 | Finish with the official course
Review your employer’s approved California food handler course materials, then take the official assessment for your card. This studio track is practice only.
NARRATION: Finish with your employer’s approved course — this lesson is practice only.

FACT: Clean then sanitize is the required sequence.
FACT: FIFO means First In, First Out.
FACT: Alameda County Environmental Health enforces local food safety rules.
