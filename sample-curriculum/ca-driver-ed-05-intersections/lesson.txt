LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Intersections
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Scanning, stopping, and moving through intersections. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Left-right-left scan
Scan both ways before you enter. At every intersection, look left, right, then left again before proceeding.
NARRATION: At every intersection, look left, right, then left again before proceeding.

SLIDE 2 | Limit lines
Stop behind the painted line. Stop with your front bumper behind the limit line so cross traffic and walkers stay clear.
NARRATION: Stop with your front bumper behind the limit line so cross traffic and walkers stay clear.

SLIDE 3 | Uncontrolled intersections
Yield to the right when arriving together. With no signs or signals, yield to vehicles already in the intersection and to the right when tied.
NARRATION: With no signs or signals, yield to vehicles already in the intersection and to the right when tied.

SLIDE 4 | T-intersections
Through traffic usually has priority. At a T-intersection, traffic on the through road generally has the right of way.
NARRATION: At a T-intersection, traffic on the through road generally has the right of way.

SLIDE 5 | Blocked intersection
Never enter if you will block it. Do not enter an intersection unless you can clear it — gridlock is illegal and dangerous.
NARRATION: Do not enter an intersection unless you can clear it — gridlock is illegal and dangerous.

SLIDE 6 | Turning into correct lane
Finish turns in the proper lane. Turn into the nearest lane of travel in your direction unless signs direct otherwise.
NARRATION: Turn into the nearest lane of travel in your direction unless signs direct otherwise.

SLIDE 7 | U-turns in California
Only where safe and legal. U-turns are restricted near hills, curves, and where signs prohibit them.
NARRATION: U-turns are restricted near hills, curves, and where signs prohibit them.

SLIDE 8 | Intersection bike boxes
Respect bike waiting areas where marked. Some cities paint bike boxes — do not stop on top of them when waiting at red.
NARRATION: Some cities paint bike boxes — do not stop on top of them when waiting at red.

SLIDE 9 | Late arrival at yellow
If already committed, clear carefully. If you are too close to stop safely on yellow, continue through and clear the intersection.
NARRATION: If you are too close to stop safely on yellow, continue through and clear the intersection.

SLIDE 10 | Stale green awareness
Expect yellow if the light has been green long. A long green may turn yellow soon — cover the brake as you approach.
NARRATION: A long green may turn yellow soon — cover the brake as you approach.

SLIDE 11 | Multi-lane turns
Stay in your lane through the turn. In multi-lane turns, stay in your lane and watch for vehicles drifting beside you.
NARRATION: In multi-lane turns, stay in your lane and watch for vehicles drifting beside you.

SLIDE 12 | Emergency in intersection
Never block for lights and sirens. If an emergency vehicle approaches, clear the intersection, then pull right.
NARRATION: If an emergency vehicle approaches, clear the intersection, then pull right.
