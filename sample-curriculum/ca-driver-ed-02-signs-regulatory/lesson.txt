LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Regulatory signs
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Stop, yield, speed, one-way, and other must-obey signs. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Sign shapes and colors
Shape and color signal meaning before you read text. Octagon means stop, triangle means yield, diamond warns, and white rectangles regulate.
NARRATION: Octagon means stop, triangle means yield, diamond warns, and white rectangles regulate.

SLIDE 2 | Stop sign complete stop
Wheels must fully stop behind the line. At a stop sign, come to a complete stop behind the limit line or crosswalk, then proceed when clear.
NARRATION: At a stop sign, come to a complete stop behind the limit line or crosswalk, then proceed when clear.

SLIDE 3 | Yield triangle
Slow and give way when others have priority. A yield sign means prepare to stop and give way to traffic and pedestrians as needed.
NARRATION: A yield sign means prepare to stop and give way to traffic and pedestrians as needed.

SLIDE 4 | Speed limit maximum
Posted speed is a maximum in ideal conditions. Never treat the posted limit as a target when weather, traffic, or visibility are poor.
NARRATION: Never treat the posted limit as a target when weather, traffic, or visibility are poor.

SLIDE 5 | Do not enter
Wrong-way entry risks a head-on crash. A do-not-enter sign means you are approaching from the wrong direction — turn around safely.
NARRATION: A do-not-enter sign means you are approaching from the wrong direction — turn around safely.

SLIDE 6 | One-way streets
Travel only in the marked direction. One-way signs and pavement arrows show the only legal direction of travel.
NARRATION: One-way signs and pavement arrows show the only legal direction of travel.

SLIDE 7 | No turn signs
Obey posted turn prohibitions. No-left-turn and no-U-turn signs override what a green light alone might allow.
NARRATION: No-left-turn and no-U-turn signs override what a green light alone might allow.

SLIDE 8 | Keep right and lane use
Lane-use signs assign which lane for which move. Lane-use control signs tell you whether a lane is for left, through, or right movements.
NARRATION: Lane-use control signs tell you whether a lane is for left, through, or right movements.

SLIDE 9 | Weight and truck limits
Some roads restrict vehicle types or weight. Bridge and road signs may ban trucks or set weight limits — plan another route if needed.
NARRATION: Bridge and road signs may ban trucks or set weight limits — plan another route if needed.

SLIDE 10 | Regulatory vs guide
White and black rules; green and blue guide. Regulatory signs create legal duties. Guide signs help you navigate but do not replace rules.
NARRATION: Regulatory signs create legal duties. Guide signs help you navigate but do not replace rules.
