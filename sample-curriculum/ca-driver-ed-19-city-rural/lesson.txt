LESSON: CA Driver Ed — City & rural driving
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Dense traffic, blind curves, and country roads. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | City scan rhythm
Eyes move; never stare. In cities, keep your eyes moving among mirrors, signals, bikes, and pedestrians.
NARRATION: In cities, keep your eyes moving among mirrors, signals, bikes, and pedestrians.

SLIDE 2 | One-way confusion
Confirm direction before you turn. City grids include one-ways — read signs before you commit to a turn.
NARRATION: City grids include one-ways — read signs before you commit to a turn.

SLIDE 3 | Delivery double-park risk
Expect doors and vans. Delivery vans may stop suddenly — leave an out and watch for opening doors.
NARRATION: Delivery vans may stop suddenly — leave an out and watch for opening doors.

SLIDE 4 | Blind intersections downtown
Creep until you can see. Where buildings block the view, creep forward until you can see cross traffic.
NARRATION: Where buildings block the view, creep forward until you can see cross traffic.

SLIDE 5 | Rural two-lane passing
Pass only with sight distance and legal lines. On two-lane roads, pass only where lines and sight distance allow — never on hills or curves.
NARRATION: On two-lane roads, pass only where lines and sight distance allow — never on hills or curves.

SLIDE 6 | Gravel and dirt shoulders
Slow; steering gets light. Loose gravel reduces traction — slow before turns and avoid hard braking.
NARRATION: Loose gravel reduces traction — slow before turns and avoid hard braking.

SLIDE 7 | Unmarked rural intersections
Assume cross traffic may not stop. Many rural intersections lack signals — slow and be ready to yield.
NARRATION: Many rural intersections lack signals — slow and be ready to yield.

SLIDE 8 | Farm equipment
Pass wide and slow. Farm equipment is wide and slow — wait for a safe gap and give plenty of space.
NARRATION: Farm equipment is wide and slow — wait for a safe gap and give plenty of space.

SLIDE 9 | Narrow bridges
Yield when required; one lane at a time. Some rural bridges are one-lane — obey yield signs and wait your turn.
NARRATION: Some rural bridges are one-lane — obey yield signs and wait your turn.

SLIDE 10 | City freeway transitions
Ramp meters and short merges. Urban freeways often have ramp meters and short merges — cooperate and match speed.
NARRATION: Urban freeways often have ramp meters and short merges — cooperate and match speed.
