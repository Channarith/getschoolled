LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Crashes & insurance
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: What to do after a collision and proof of insurance. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Stop and secure the scene
Never leave the scene of a crash. If involved in a crash, stop, turn on hazards if safe, and move out of traffic when you can.
NARRATION: If involved in a crash, stop, turn on hazards if safe, and move out of traffic when you can.

SLIDE 2 | Call for injuries
People first, then property. Call 911 when anyone is hurt or when the crash blocks a roadway.
NARRATION: Call 911 when anyone is hurt or when the crash blocks a roadway.

SLIDE 3 | Exchange information
Names, contacts, license, insurance. Exchange names, contact info, driver license numbers, and insurance details with others involved.
NARRATION: Exchange names, contact info, driver license numbers, and insurance details with others involved.

SLIDE 4 | Do not argue fault
Facts and photos beat roadside debate. Do not argue fault at the scene — document facts and let insurers investigate.
NARRATION: Do not argue fault at the scene — document facts and let insurers investigate.

SLIDE 5 | Hit and run response
Note details; report promptly. If the other driver flees, note plate, vehicle, and direction, then report to police.
NARRATION: If the other driver flees, note plate, vehicle, and direction, then report to police.

SLIDE 6 | Proof of insurance
Carry evidence of financial responsibility. California requires proof of financial responsibility — know how to show it digitally or on paper.
NARRATION: California requires proof of financial responsibility — know how to show it digitally or on paper.

SLIDE 7 | Uninsured motorist awareness
Protect yourself with coverage. Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage helps if the other driver lacks insurance.
NARRATION: Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage helps if the other driver lacks insurance.

SLIDE 8 | Report when required
Some crashes must be reported to DMV or police. Report crashes that meet injury or damage thresholds as required by California law.
NARRATION: Report crashes that meet injury or damage thresholds as required by California law.

SLIDE 9 | Witnesses and cameras
Gather neutral evidence. Ask witnesses for contact info and note nearby cameras that may have recorded the crash.
NARRATION: Ask witnesses for contact info and note nearby cameras that may have recorded the crash.

SLIDE 10 | Aftercare and follow-up
Seek medical care for delayed symptoms. Some injuries appear later — seek care and notify your insurer promptly.
NARRATION: Some injuries appear later — seek care and notify your insurer promptly.

FACT: Always verify rules in the current California Driver's Handbook.
