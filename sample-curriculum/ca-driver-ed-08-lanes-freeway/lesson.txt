LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Lanes & freeway driving
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Merging, passing, HOV, and lane discipline. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Mirror-signal-shoulder
Every lane change needs a shoulder check. Check mirrors, signal, glance over your shoulder, then move smoothly into the gap.
NARRATION: Check mirrors, signal, glance over your shoulder, then move smoothly into the gap.

SLIDE 2 | Freeway merge zipper
Alternate gaps when lanes end. When two lanes merge, take turns — zipper merge reduces last-second cut-ins.
NARRATION: When two lanes merge, take turns — zipper merge reduces last-second cut-ins.

SLIDE 3 | Left lane for passing
Pass, then return right when safe. Use the left lane to pass, then move right so faster traffic can continue.
NARRATION: Use the left lane to pass, then move right so faster traffic can continue.

SLIDE 4 | HOV carpool lanes
Meet occupancy or enter legally. HOV lanes require the posted number of occupants or an authorized pass.
NARRATION: HOV lanes require the posted number of occupants or an authorized pass.

SLIDE 5 | Exit early preparation
Change lanes early for your exit. Read guide signs early and move toward your exit lane with time to spare.
NARRATION: Read guide signs early and move toward your exit lane with time to spare.

SLIDE 6 | No stopping on freeway
Only stop fully off the roadway if you must. Do not stop in a travel lane. If you must stop, get fully onto the shoulder and turn on hazards.
NARRATION: Do not stop in a travel lane. If you must stop, get fully onto the shoulder and turn on hazards.

SLIDE 7 | Weave zones
Expect conflict where enter and exit meet. In weave areas, scan both sides and keep a flexible gap for entering and exiting cars.
NARRATION: In weave areas, scan both sides and keep a flexible gap for entering and exiting cars.

SLIDE 8 | Passing on the right
Legal in some multi-lane cases — still careful. Passing on the right may be allowed on multi-lane roads but watch for right-turning vehicles.
NARRATION: Passing on the right may be allowed on multi-lane roads but watch for right-turning vehicles.

SLIDE 9 | Truck climbing lanes
Use extra lanes to pass slow trucks on hills. Climbing lanes let faster traffic pass — return right after you clear the truck.
NARRATION: Climbing lanes let faster traffic pass — return right after you clear the truck.

SLIDE 10 | Freeway hypnosis
Break monotony on long trips. On long drives, vary focus, take breaks, and avoid staring at one point ahead.
NARRATION: On long drives, vary focus, take breaks, and avoid staring at one point ahead.

SLIDE 11 | Wrong-way driver response
Pull right, slow, warn — never swerve into them. If you see a wrong-way driver, move right, slow, honk, and flash lights.
NARRATION: If you see a wrong-way driver, move right, slow, honk, and flash lights.

SLIDE 12 | Lane splitting awareness
Expect motorcycles between lanes in CA. California allows motorcycle lane splitting when done safely — check mirrors before changing lanes.
NARRATION: California allows motorcycle lane splitting when done safely — check mirrors before changing lanes.

FACT: Always verify rules in the current California Driver's Handbook.
