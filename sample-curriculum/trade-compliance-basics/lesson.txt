LESSON: Trade Compliance Essentials
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: corporate
TRACK: Trade
LEVEL: Role-based required
ROLE: Ops, sales, logistics, engineering
DELIVERY: 50–60 min live class
FIT: Teams that ship products, share technical data, or work with international partners.
SUMMARY: Import/export basics, denied parties, classification, and when to escalate to trade compliance.

SLIDE 1 | What Trade Compliance Covers
Trade compliance ensures products, software, and technical data move across
borders legally. It includes classification, licensing, sanctions screening,
and accurate customs declarations. Violations can bring heavy fines and
shipment delays.
NARRATION: Trade compliance ensures products, software, and technical data move across borders legally.

SLIDE 2 | Know Your Transaction
Before shipping or sharing controlled information, confirm the product
classification, destination country, end user, and end use. Incomplete
paperwork is a red flag. When unsure, pause and escalate.
NARRATION: Before shipping or sharing controlled information, confirm the product classification, destination country, end user, and end use.

SLIDE 3 | Denied Party Screening
Governments publish lists of restricted parties. Screen customers, partners,
and freight forwarders before engaging. Matches must be reviewed by compliance
— never ignore a screening hit.
NARRATION: Governments publish lists of restricted parties.

SLIDE 4 | Accurate Records
Keep commercial invoices, packing lists, licenses, and correspondence
organized. Regulators expect you to reconstruct a transaction years later.
Never invent HS codes or undervalue goods.
NARRATION: Keep commercial invoices, packing lists, licenses, and correspondence organized.

SLIDE 5 | Escalate Early
If a customer asks to remove origin marks, route through unusual countries, or
receive controlled designs without paperwork, stop and contact trade
compliance. Speed is never worth an export violation.
NARRATION: If a customer asks to remove origin marks, route through unusual countries, or receive controlled designs without paperwork, stop and contact trade compliance.
