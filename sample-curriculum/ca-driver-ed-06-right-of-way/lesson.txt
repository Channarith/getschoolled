DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session

SUMMARY: Who goes first — and when to yield anyway. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Right-of-way is given, not taken
Yielding prevents crashes even if you are 'right'. Having the right-of-way never means forcing others to stop — yield to avoid a collision.
NARRATION: Having the right-of-way never means forcing others to stop — yield to avoid a collision.

SLIDE 2 | Four-way stop order
First to arrive goes first. At all-way stops, the first vehicle to arrive proceeds first; ties yield to the right.
NARRATION: At all-way stops, the first vehicle to arrive proceeds first; ties yield to the right.

SLIDE 3 | Pedestrians always
Yield to pedestrians in crosswalks. Drivers must yield to pedestrians in marked or unmarked crosswalks.
NARRATION: Drivers must yield to pedestrians in marked or unmarked crosswalks.

SLIDE 4 | Blind pedestrians
Stop and give space to white cane or guide dog users. Yield extra space and time to pedestrians using a white cane or guide dog.
NARRATION: Yield extra space and time to pedestrians using a white cane or guide dog.

SLIDE 5 | Funeral processions
Yield to processions where required. Do not cut into a funeral procession; yield and proceed only when safe and lawful.
NARRATION: Do not cut into a funeral procession; yield and proceed only when safe and lawful.

SLIDE 6 | Transit buses pulling out
Yield when required by local rules. In some areas you must yield to buses re-entering traffic — watch for signals.
NARRATION: In some areas you must yield to buses re-entering traffic — watch for signals.

SLIDE 7 | Driveway vs sidewalk
Sidewalk users go first. When leaving a driveway, stop and yield to pedestrians on the sidewalk before entering the street.
NARRATION: When leaving a driveway, stop and yield to pedestrians on the sidewalk before entering the street.

SLIDE 8 | Private road entry
Yield to all traffic on the public road. Entering from an alley or private road, yield to vehicles and pedestrians on the public roadway.
NARRATION: Entering from an alley or private road, yield to vehicles and pedestrians on the public roadway.

SLIDE 9 | Roundabout right-of-way
Yield to traffic already circulating. At roundabouts, yield to vehicles in the circle, then enter when there is a gap.
NARRATION: At roundabouts, yield to vehicles in the circle, then enter when there is a gap.

SLIDE 10 | When in doubt, yield
Uncertainty means wait. If you are unsure who should go, wait — a few seconds of caution beats a crash.
NARRATION: If you are unsure who should go, wait — a few seconds of caution beats a crash.
