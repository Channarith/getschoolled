SLIDE 1 | Yellow diamond meaning
Slow and prepare — not always a full stop. Yellow diamond warning signs alert you to hazards ahead so you can adjust early.
NARRATION: Yellow diamond warning signs alert you to hazards ahead so you can adjust early.

SLIDE 2 | Curve and winding road
Reduce speed before the curve, not in it. Curve signs mean ease off the gas before you enter and stay in your lane.
NARRATION: Curve signs mean ease off the gas before you enter and stay in your lane.

SLIDE 3 | Merge ahead
Watch for vehicles entering your lane. Merge warning signs remind you to scan and create gaps for entering traffic.
NARRATION: Merge warning signs remind you to scan and create gaps for entering traffic.

SLIDE 4 | Pedestrian crossing ahead
Expect people in or near the roadway. Pedestrian warning signs mean slow down and be ready to stop for walkers.
NARRATION: Pedestrian warning signs mean slow down and be ready to stop for walkers.

SLIDE 5 | Bicycle warning
Share space with cyclists ahead. Bike warning signs mean scan for cyclists and give them room.
NARRATION: Bike warning signs mean scan for cyclists and give them room.

SLIDE 6 | Slippery when wet
Reduce speed on wet pavement. Slippery-road warnings mean tires lose grip — slow down and avoid sudden inputs.
NARRATION: Slippery-road warnings mean tires lose grip — slow down and avoid sudden inputs.

SLIDE 7 | Deer and animal crossing
Scan shoulders in rural areas. Animal crossing signs mean wildlife may enter the road, especially at dusk.
NARRATION: Animal crossing signs mean wildlife may enter the road, especially at dusk.

SLIDE 8 | Hill and downgrade
Control speed on long descents. Downgrade signs mean use a lower gear and avoid riding the brakes.
NARRATION: Downgrade signs mean use a lower gear and avoid riding the brakes.

SLIDE 9 | Soft shoulder
Stay on pavement if the shoulder is soft. Soft-shoulder warnings mean leaving the pavement can pull you off the road.
NARRATION: Soft-shoulder warnings mean leaving the pavement can pull you off the road.

SLIDE 10 | Two-way traffic ahead
Opposing traffic begins after a divided section. Two-way traffic signs mean keep right and expect oncoming vehicles.
NARRATION: Two-way traffic signs mean keep right and expect oncoming vehicles.
