LESSON: US Export Control Regulations
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: corporate
TRACK: Trade
LEVEL: Role-based required
ROLE: Engineering, product, sales, logistics
DELIVERY: 60–75 min live class
FIT: Teams handling controlled technology, software, or international customers.
SUMMARY: EAR and ITAR basics, deemed exports, classification, licenses, and red flags for US export controls.

SLIDE 1 | What Are Export Controls?
US export controls restrict the transfer of certain goods, software, and
technology to foreign persons or destinations. The Export Administration
Regulations (EAR) and International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) are the
primary frameworks. Controls exist for national security and foreign policy
reasons.
NARRATION: US export controls restrict the transfer of certain goods, software, and technology to foreign persons or destinations.

SLIDE 2 | Exports Are More Than Shipments
An export can be a physical shipment, an email with controlled files, a cloud
share, or a conversation that releases technical data to a foreign person in
the US (a deemed export). Treat technical disclosures with the same care as
shipping a product.
NARRATION: An export can be a physical shipment, an email with controlled files, a cloud share, or a conversation that releases technical data to a foreign person in the US (a deemed export).

SLIDE 3 | Classification and Licensing
Items may be classified under ECCNs or USML categories. Some items are EAR99;
others need licenses for certain countries or end uses. Classification
mistakes are common — escalate borderline cases.
NARRATION: Items may be classified under ECCNs or USML categories.

SLIDE 4 | Restricted Destinations and End Uses
Sanctions and embargoes change. Military end uses, nuclear activities, and
certain destinations trigger extra scrutiny. Always check current lists and
involve export compliance.
NARRATION: Sanctions and embargoes change.

SLIDE 5 | Employee Responsibilities
Do not send controlled files abroad without clearance. Confirm
citizenship/status requirements for lab access when required. Report suspected
violations promptly. Compliance is everyone's job.
NARRATION: Do not send controlled files abroad without clearance.
