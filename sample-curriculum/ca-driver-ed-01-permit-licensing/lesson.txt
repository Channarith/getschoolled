SLIDE 1 | Study aid, not DMV course
Confirm every rule in the official handbook. This track is practice only. Always verify rules in the current California Driver's Handbook.
NARRATION: This track is practice only. Always verify rules in the current California Driver's Handbook.

SLIDE 2 | Who needs a permit
Instruction permit before solo provisional license. Most new drivers start with an instruction permit, then a provisional license after requirements are met.
NARRATION: Most new drivers start with an instruction permit, then a provisional license after requirements are met.

SLIDE 3 | Age and driver education
Teens usually need education plus parent consent. Typical teen applicants need driver education, practice hours, and a parent or guardian signature.
NARRATION: Typical teen applicants need driver education, practice hours, and a parent or guardian signature.

SLIDE 4 | Vision and knowledge tests
Pass vision and knowledge before the permit issues. Expect a vision screening and a knowledge test covering signs, rules, and sharing the road.
NARRATION: Expect a vision screening and a knowledge test covering signs, rules, and sharing the road.

SLIDE 5 | Practice hour log
Log day and night supervised hours. California requires substantial supervised practice, including night hours, before the road test.
NARRATION: California requires substantial supervised practice, including night hours, before the road test.

SLIDE 6 | Supervising driver rules
Licensed adult in the front seat while practicing. With a permit, a qualified licensed driver must supervise from the front passenger seat.
NARRATION: With a permit, a qualified licensed driver must supervise from the front passenger seat.

SLIDE 7 | Provisional passenger limits
First-year passenger restrictions apply. Provisional holders usually may carry only immediate family unless a qualified supervisor is present.
NARRATION: Provisional holders usually may carry only immediate family unless a qualified supervisor is present.

SLIDE 8 | Night curfew for teens
Honor provisional nighttime limits. Provisional drivers face night driving limits unless an exception applies.
NARRATION: Provisional drivers face night driving limits unless an exception applies.

SLIDE 9 | License classes overview
Class C covers most passenger cars. Most learners seek a Class C license. Commercial and motorcycle classes have separate requirements.
NARRATION: Most learners seek a Class C license. Commercial and motorcycle classes have separate requirements.

SLIDE 10 | Keep documents current
Carry license and insurance proof when you drive. Drive only with a valid license or permit and carry proof of financial responsibility when required.
NARRATION: Drive only with a valid license or permit and carry proof of financial responsibility when required.
