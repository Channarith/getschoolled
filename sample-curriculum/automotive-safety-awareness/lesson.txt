LESSON: Automotive Safety Awareness
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: corporate
TRACK: Automotive
LEVEL: Foundational
ROLE: Service, fleet, and shop teams
DELIVERY: 40–50 min live class
FIT: Technicians, fleet operators, and anyone working around vehicles and shop equipment.
SUMMARY: Shop hazards, lift safety, battery/EV awareness, PPE, and safe vehicle movement practices.

SLIDE 1 | Shop Hazards Overview
Automotive workplaces involve moving vehicles, heavy lifts, flammable fluids,
batteries, and powered tools. Situational awareness prevents crush, burn, and
chemical injuries.
NARRATION: Automotive workplaces involve moving vehicles, heavy lifts, flammable fluids, batteries, and powered tools.

SLIDE 2 | Lifts and Jacks
Use rated equipment, secure vehicles before working underneath, and never rely
on a jack alone. Inspect stands and locks every time.
NARRATION: Use rated equipment, secure vehicles before working underneath, and never rely on a jack alone.

SLIDE 3 | Batteries and High Voltage
Treat batteries as energy sources. Wear eye protection, avoid shorting
terminals, and follow EV high-voltage procedures when applicable. Assume
orange cables mean danger.
NARRATION: Treat batteries as energy sources.

SLIDE 4 | Chemicals and Ventilation
Fuels, solvents, and exhaust fumes require ventilation and correct PPE. Store
flammables properly and clean spills promptly.
NARRATION: Fuels, solvents, and exhaust fumes require ventilation and correct PPE.

SLIDE 5 | Moving Vehicles Safely
Use spotters, check blind spots, and keep pedestrians clear. Keys control
responsibility — know who is authorized to move vehicles.
NARRATION: Use spotters, check blind spots, and keep pedestrians clear.

FACT: Automotive workplaces involve moving vehicles, heavy lifts, flammable fluids, batteries, and powered tools.
FACT: Use rated equipment, secure vehicles before working underneath, and never rely on a jack alone.
FACT: Treat batteries as energy sources.
FACT: Fuels, solvents, and exhaust fumes require ventilation and correct PPE.
FACT: Use spotters, check blind spots, and keep pedestrians clear.
