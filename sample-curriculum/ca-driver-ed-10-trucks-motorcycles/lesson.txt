LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Trucks & motorcycles
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general

JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: No-zones, wide turns, and motorcycle visibility. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Truck no-zones
If you cannot see their mirrors, they cannot see you. Trucks have large blind spots on all sides — avoid lingering beside or close behind.
NARRATION: Trucks have large blind spots on all sides — avoid lingering beside or close behind.

SLIDE 2 | Truck stopping distance
Never cut in and brake after passing. After passing a truck, leave a large gap before you pull in — they need long stopping distance.
NARRATION: After passing a truck, leave a large gap before you pull in — they need long stopping distance.

SLIDE 3 | Wide right turns
Give trucks room to swing left before turning right. Trucks may move left before a right turn — do not squeeze inside.
NARRATION: Trucks may move left before a right turn — do not squeeze inside.

SLIDE 4 | Mountain downgrades
Trucks may use runaway ramps — stay clear. On steep grades, give trucks space and never block a runaway truck ramp.
NARRATION: On steep grades, give trucks space and never block a runaway truck ramp.

SLIDE 5 | Look twice for motorcycles
Motorcycles hide in blind spots. Shoulder-check for motorcycles before every lane change — they are easy to miss.
NARRATION: Shoulder-check for motorcycles before every lane change — they are easy to miss.

SLIDE 6 | Full lane for motorcycles
Give motorcycles a full lane width. Do not share a lane side-by-side with a motorcycle unless lane splitting is occurring safely.
NARRATION: Do not share a lane side-by-side with a motorcycle unless lane splitting is occurring safely.

SLIDE 7 | Motorcycle following gap
Leave extra space behind bikes. Motorcycles can stop quickly — keep a generous following distance.
NARRATION: Motorcycles can stop quickly — keep a generous following distance.

SLIDE 8 | Lane splitting safe response
Hold your lane; do not squeeze the rider. If a motorcycle splits lanes near you, hold a steady line and avoid sudden moves.
NARRATION: If a motorcycle splits lanes near you, hold a steady line and avoid sudden moves.

SLIDE 9 | Filter at lights
Expect bikes between stopped cars. At red lights, motorcycles may filter forward — check before you inch up.
NARRATION: At red lights, motorcycles may filter forward — check before you inch up.

SLIDE 10 | Truck wind blast
Grip the wheel when passing large vehicles. Passing trucks can buffet your car — keep both hands on the wheel and a steady speed.
NARRATION: Passing trucks can buffet your car — keep both hands on the wheel and a steady speed.
