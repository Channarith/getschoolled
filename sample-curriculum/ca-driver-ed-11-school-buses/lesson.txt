LESSON: CA Driver Ed — School buses & school zones
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Red lights, stop arms, and child unpredictability. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Red lights and stop arm
Both directions stop on undivided roads. When a school bus shows red flashing lights and a stop arm on an undivided road, traffic both ways must stop.
NARRATION: When a school bus shows red flashing lights and a stop arm on an undivided road, traffic both ways must stop.

SLIDE 2 | Divided highway exception
Only same-direction traffic stops on divided roads. On a divided highway, usually only traffic traveling the same direction as the bus must stop.
NARRATION: On a divided highway, usually only traffic traveling the same direction as the bus must stop.

SLIDE 3 | Yellow bus lights
Prepare to stop — children may load soon. Flashing yellow bus lights mean slow down and prepare to stop.
NARRATION: Flashing yellow bus lights mean slow down and prepare to stop.

SLIDE 4 | Children darting out
Expect kids from behind the bus. Children may cross in front of or dash from behind a bus — wait until clear.
NARRATION: Children may cross in front of or dash from behind a bus — wait until clear.

SLIDE 5 | School zone flashing beacons
Obey reduced limits when lights flash. Flashing school-zone beacons often activate lower speed limits — obey them.
NARRATION: Flashing school-zone beacons often activate lower speed limits — obey them.

SLIDE 6 | Crossing guards
Follow the guard's directions. Crossing guards control the crosswalk — stop when they signal and wait for their clearance.
NARRATION: Crossing guards control the crosswalk — stop when they signal and wait for their clearance.

SLIDE 7 | Arrival and dismissal peaks
Extra caution at bell times. Traffic near schools peaks at arrival and dismissal — expect congestion and double-parked cars.
NARRATION: Traffic near schools peaks at arrival and dismissal — expect congestion and double-parked cars.

SLIDE 8 | No passing near schools
Do not pass vehicles stopped for kids. Never pass a vehicle that is stopped for pedestrians in a school crosswalk.
NARRATION: Never pass a vehicle that is stopped for pedestrians in a school crosswalk.

SLIDE 9 | Field trip buses
Same stop rules apply to school activity buses. Activity buses loading students generally follow the same stop-arm rules — treat them seriously.
NARRATION: Activity buses loading students generally follow the same stop-arm rules — treat them seriously.

SLIDE 10 | Parking near schools
Keep crosswalks and bus zones clear. Do not park in bus loading zones or block crosswalks near schools.
NARRATION: Do not park in bus loading zones or block crosswalks near schools.

FACT: Always verify rules in the current California Driver's Handbook.
