LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Night & weather
LANGUAGE: en

JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Headlights, fog, rain, and hydroplaning. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Headlight hours
Lights on from dusk to dawn and in poor visibility. Use headlights from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise and whenever visibility is poor.
NARRATION: Use headlights from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise and whenever visibility is poor.

SLIDE 2 | Dim high beams
Dim for oncoming and when following. Dim high beams for oncoming traffic and when you are following another vehicle closely.
NARRATION: Dim high beams for oncoming traffic and when you are following another vehicle closely.

SLIDE 3 | Overdriving headlights
If you cannot stop within what you see, you are too fast. At night, drive so you can stop within the distance your headlights reveal.
NARRATION: At night, drive so you can stop within the distance your headlights reveal.

SLIDE 4 | Fog low beams
High beams bounce back in fog. In fog use low beams — high beams reflect and worsen glare.
NARRATION: In fog use low beams — high beams reflect and worsen glare.

SLIDE 5 | Rain first minutes
Roads are slickest just as rain begins. Oil and water mix when rain starts — slow down immediately in the first minutes of rain.
NARRATION: Oil and water mix when rain starts — slow down immediately in the first minutes of rain.

SLIDE 6 | Hydroplaning response
Ease off the gas; steer straight. If you hydroplane, ease off the gas and steer straight until the tires regain grip — avoid sudden braking.
NARRATION: If you hydroplane, ease off the gas and steer straight until the tires regain grip — avoid sudden braking.

SLIDE 7 | Standing water
Slow before the puddle. Standing water can hide potholes and cause hydroplaning — slow before you hit it.
NARRATION: Standing water can hide potholes and cause hydroplaning — slow before you hit it.

SLIDE 8 | Wind gusts
Two hands; watch high-profile vehicles. In strong wind, keep both hands on the wheel and give trucks and vans extra space.
NARRATION: In strong wind, keep both hands on the wheel and give trucks and vans extra space.

SLIDE 9 | Ice and black ice
Bridges freeze first. Bridges and shaded spots freeze first — slow early and avoid sudden inputs.
NARRATION: Bridges and shaded spots freeze first — slow early and avoid sudden inputs.

SLIDE 10 | Sun glare
Visor, clean windshield, slower speed. Low sun can blind you — use the visor, keep glass clean, and slow until you can see.
NARRATION: Low sun can blind you — use the visor, keep glass clean, and slow until you can see.
