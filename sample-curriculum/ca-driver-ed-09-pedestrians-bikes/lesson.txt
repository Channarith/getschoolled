LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Pedestrians & bicycles
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Crosswalks, three-foot pass, and door-zone safety. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Marked crosswalk duty
Stop for people in the crosswalk. Yield to pedestrians in marked crosswalks and do not pass a vehicle stopped at a crosswalk.
NARRATION: Yield to pedestrians in marked crosswalks and do not pass a vehicle stopped at a crosswalk.

SLIDE 2 | Unmarked crosswalks
Corners are crosswalks too. Intersections have crosswalks even without paint — yield to people crossing.
NARRATION: Intersections have crosswalks even without paint — yield to people crossing.

SLIDE 3 | Mid-block joggers
Expect people outside crosswalks. Scan for joggers and walkers mid-block, especially near parks and schools.
NARRATION: Scan for joggers and walkers mid-block, especially near parks and schools.

SLIDE 4 | Three-foot bike pass
Leave at least three feet when passing. When overtaking a bicycle, leave at least three feet of space or wait until you can.
NARRATION: When overtaking a bicycle, leave at least three feet of space or wait until you can.

SLIDE 5 | Bike lanes respect
Do not drive or park in bike lanes. Bike lanes are for bicycles — do not drive, stop, or park in them except where allowed.
NARRATION: Bike lanes are for bicycles — do not drive, stop, or park in them except where allowed.

SLIDE 6 | Dutch reach dooring
Look back before opening your door. Open the door with the far hand so you naturally look for cyclists first.
NARRATION: Open the door with the far hand so you naturally look for cyclists first.

SLIDE 7 | Right hook risk
Check for bikes before right turns. Before turning right, shoulder-check for cyclists coming up on your right.
NARRATION: Before turning right, shoulder-check for cyclists coming up on your right.

SLIDE 8 | Left cross risk
Watch bikes when turning left across a lane. Left turns across bike lanes need a clear gap — bikes move faster than you think.
NARRATION: Left turns across bike lanes need a clear gap — bikes move faster than you think.

SLIDE 9 | Shared-use paths
Yield where paths meet roads. Where a shared path crosses a road, slow and yield according to signs and markings.
NARRATION: Where a shared path crosses a road, slow and yield according to signs and markings.

SLIDE 10 | Night bike lights
Expect poorly lit riders — slow down. At night, scan carefully; not every cyclist has bright lights.
NARRATION: At night, scan carefully; not every cyclist has bright lights.
