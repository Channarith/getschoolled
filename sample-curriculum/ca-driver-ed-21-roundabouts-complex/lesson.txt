LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Roundabouts & complex moves
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general

LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Circles, multi-lane choices, and unusual controls. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Single-lane roundabout
Yield, enter, signal to exit. Yield to circulating traffic, enter when clear, and signal before your exit.
NARRATION: Yield to circulating traffic, enter when clear, and signal before your exit.

SLIDE 2 | Multi-lane roundabout
Choose the lane before you enter. Pick the correct lane for your exit before entering a multi-lane roundabout.
NARRATION: Pick the correct lane for your exit before entering a multi-lane roundabout.

SLIDE 3 | Do not stop in the circle
Keep moving once inside unless blocked. Avoid stopping in the circulating roadway except to avoid a collision.
NARRATION: Avoid stopping in the circulating roadway except to avoid a collision.

SLIDE 4 | Trucks in roundabouts
Give large vehicles extra room. Trucks may use more than one lane in a roundabout — give them space.
NARRATION: Trucks may use more than one lane in a roundabout — give them space.

SLIDE 5 | Pedestrians at roundabouts
Yield at each crosswalk. Yield to pedestrians at the entry and exit crosswalks of a roundabout.
NARRATION: Yield to pedestrians at the entry and exit crosswalks of a roundabout.

SLIDE 6 | Traffic circles vs roundabouts
Follow the specific signs on site. Older traffic circles may differ from modern roundabouts — obey the signs you see.
NARRATION: Older traffic circles may differ from modern roundabouts — obey the signs you see.

SLIDE 7 | Jughandle turns
Some lefts are made by turning right first. Jughandle designs send left-turning traffic to the right first — follow the markings.
NARRATION: Jughandle designs send left-turning traffic to the right first — follow the markings.

SLIDE 8 | Protected-permissive lefts
Green ball means yield to oncoming. A green ball left turn is permissive — yield to oncoming traffic; a green arrow is protected.
NARRATION: A green ball left turn is permissive — yield to oncoming traffic; a green arrow is protected.

SLIDE 9 | Dual right-turn lanes
Stay in your lane through the turn. Where two lanes may turn right, stay in your lane and watch for merges after the turn.
NARRATION: Where two lanes may turn right, stay in your lane and watch for merges after the turn.

SLIDE 10 | Complex signal timing
Wait for your arrow; do not jump. At complex signals, wait for the indication that applies to your movement — do not assume.
NARRATION: At complex signals, wait for the indication that applies to your movement — do not assume.
