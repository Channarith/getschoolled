LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Parking & maneuvers
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Curb colors, hills, parallel parking, and clearances. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Colored curb meanings
Read curb colors before you leave the car. Red, blue, green, yellow, and white curbs each carry different parking rules — learn your city's code.
NARRATION: Red, blue, green, yellow, and white curbs each carry different parking rules — learn your city's code.

SLIDE 2 | Hydrant clearance
Stay far enough from fire hydrants. Do not park too close to a fire hydrant — emergency crews need immediate access.
NARRATION: Do not park too close to a fire hydrant — emergency crews need immediate access.

SLIDE 3 | Crosswalk and driveway clearance
Keep crossings and driveways open. Parking too close to crosswalks, stop signs, or driveways blocks sight lines and access.
NARRATION: Parking too close to crosswalks, stop signs, or driveways blocks sight lines and access.

SLIDE 4 | Hill parking uphill curb
Turn wheels away from the curb uphill. When parking uphill with a curb, turn wheels away from the curb and set the parking brake.
NARRATION: When parking uphill with a curb, turn wheels away from the curb and set the parking brake.

SLIDE 5 | Hill parking downhill curb
Turn wheels toward the curb downhill. When parking downhill with a curb, turn wheels toward the curb so a roll hits the curb.
NARRATION: When parking downhill with a curb, turn wheels toward the curb so a roll hits the curb.

SLIDE 6 | Parallel parking steps
Signal, align, reverse slowly, straighten. Signal, align with the car ahead, reverse into the space, then straighten and center.
NARRATION: Signal, align with the car ahead, reverse into the space, then straighten and center.

SLIDE 7 | Perpendicular parking
Center in the stall; wheels straight. Pull into the stall centered with wheels straight so you do not stick out into the aisle.
NARRATION: Pull into the stall centered with wheels straight so you do not stick out into the aisle.

SLIDE 8 | Backing out of stalls
Creep and yield to aisle traffic. Back out slowly, check both sides, and yield to pedestrians and cars in the aisle.
NARRATION: Back out slowly, check both sides, and yield to pedestrians and cars in the aisle.

SLIDE 9 | No double parking
Never block a travel lane to wait. Double parking blocks traffic and emergency access — find a legal space.
NARRATION: Double parking blocks traffic and emergency access — find a legal space.

SLIDE 10 | Disabled parking rules
Only with a valid placard or plates. Blue curb and disabled stalls require a valid placard or plates — misuse is heavily fined.
NARRATION: Blue curb and disabled stalls require a valid placard or plates — misuse is heavily fined.

FACT: Always verify rules in the current California Driver's Handbook.
