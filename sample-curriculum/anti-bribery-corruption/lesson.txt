LESSON: Anti-Bribery and Corruption
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: corporate
TRACK: Compliance
LEVEL: Required annual training
ROLE: Sales, procurement, finance, leadership
DELIVERY: 45–60 min live class
FIT: Employees who interact with customers, suppliers, or government officials.
SUMMARY: FCPA and UK Bribery Act basics, gifts and hospitality red flags, third-party risk, and how to say no.

SLIDE 1 | What Is Bribery?
Bribery is offering, giving, requesting, or accepting anything of value to
improperly influence a business or government decision. It can be cash, gifts,
travel, jobs for relatives, or charitable donations used as cover. Both public
and commercial bribery create risk.
NARRATION: Bribery is offering, giving, requesting, or accepting anything of value to improperly influence a business or government decision.

SLIDE 2 | Key Laws
The US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act prohibit
corrupt payments and require accurate books and records. Companies can be
liable for actions of employees and third parties. Penalties include fines,
jail time, and debarment.
NARRATION: The US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act prohibit corrupt payments and require accurate books and records.

SLIDE 3 | Gifts and Hospitality
Modest, transparent hospitality may be allowed by policy. Lavish travel, cash
equivalents, or gifts timed to a bid are red flags. Get pre-approval when
required and record what was given.
NARRATION: Modest, transparent hospitality may be allowed by policy.

SLIDE 4 | Third Parties
Agents, distributors, and consultants can create bribery risk. Screen
partners, use written contracts, and watch for unusual commissions or payment
requests to personal accounts.
NARRATION: Agents, distributors, and consultants can create bribery risk.

SLIDE 5 | Speak Up
Refuse improper requests clearly, document the interaction, and report to
Compliance. Retaliation for good-faith reports is prohibited. When in doubt,
ask before you act.
NARRATION: Refuse improper requests clearly, document the interaction, and report to Compliance.

FACT: The US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act prohibit corrupt payments and require accurate books and records.
FACT: Modest, transparent hospitality may be allowed by policy.
FACT: Agents, distributors, and consultants can create bribery risk.
FACT: Refuse improper requests clearly, document the interaction, and report to Compliance.
