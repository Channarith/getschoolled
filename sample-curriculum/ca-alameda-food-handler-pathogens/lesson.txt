SLIDE 3 | Norovirus deep dive
Norovirus is highly contagious and a leading cause of foodborne illness from infected food handlers. It spreads through vomit particles, feces, and contaminated ready-to-eat foods. Exclusion, ruthless handwashing, and staying off the line when sick are the main defenses — cooking helps less for foods served raw.
