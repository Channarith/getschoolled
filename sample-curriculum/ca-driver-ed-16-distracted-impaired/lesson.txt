SLIDE 1 | Handheld phone ban
Handheld calling and texting while driving are illegal. California restricts handheld phone use while driving — use hands-free or pull over safely.
NARRATION: California restricts handheld phone use while driving — use hands-free or pull over safely.

SLIDE 2 | Hands-free still distracts
Conversation still steals attention. Hands-free is safer legally but still distracts — keep calls short or pull over for complex talks.
NARRATION: Hands-free is safer legally but still distracts — keep calls short or pull over for complex talks.

SLIDE 3 | Dashboard screens
Set navigation before you move. Program navigation and playlists before you drive — do not stare at screens in motion.
NARRATION: Program navigation and playlists before you drive — do not stare at screens in motion.

SLIDE 4 | Eating and grooming
Pull over for tasks that need hands or eyes. Eating, makeup, and reaching for dropped items take eyes and hands off the driving task.
NARRATION: Eating, makeup, and reaching for dropped items take eyes and hands off the driving task.

SLIDE 5 | Passenger distraction
You control the cabin. Ask passengers to keep noise down when you need focus — the driver owns the cabin safety.
NARRATION: Ask passengers to keep noise down when you need focus — the driver owns the cabin safety.

SLIDE 6 | Drowsy driving
Sleep beats caffeine for fatigue. If you are drowsy, stop and rest — microsleeps at highway speed are deadly.
NARRATION: If you are drowsy, stop and rest — microsleeps at highway speed are deadly.

SLIDE 7 | BAC 0.08 adult limit
Adults 21+ face 0.08 percent BAC limit. For drivers 21 and older, the BAC limit is 0.08 percent — impairment starts earlier.
NARRATION: For drivers 21 and older, the BAC limit is 0.08 percent — impairment starts earlier.

SLIDE 8 | Zero tolerance under 21
Any measurable alcohol can mean sanctions. Drivers under 21 face zero-tolerance rules for alcohol — arrange another ride.
NARRATION: Drivers under 21 face zero-tolerance rules for alcohol — arrange another ride.

SLIDE 9 | Medication impairment
Read labels; ask a pharmacist. Some prescriptions and over-the-counter meds impair driving — check warnings before you drive.
NARRATION: Some prescriptions and over-the-counter meds impair driving — check warnings before you drive.

SLIDE 10 | Cannabis and driving
Impaired is impaired — wait it out. Cannabis can impair judgment and reaction time. Do not drive until you are fully sober.
NARRATION: Cannabis can impair judgment and reaction time. Do not drive until you are fully sober.
