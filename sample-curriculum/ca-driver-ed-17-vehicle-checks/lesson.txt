LESSON: CA Driver Ed — Vehicle safety checks
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: general
TRACK: Certifications
LEVEL: Certification prep
JURISDICTION: us-ca
PREP_ONLY: true
DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session
FIT: Learners preparing for the California DMV knowledge / permit test.
SUMMARY: Pre-drive inspections and warning lights. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Walk-around check
Look for leaks, low tires, and obstacles. Before you drive, walk around the car — check tires, lights, and anything behind the bumper.
NARRATION: Before you drive, walk around the car — check tires, lights, and anything behind the bumper.

SLIDE 2 | Tire pressure and tread
Bald or soft tires fail in emergencies. Check tread and pressure regularly — underinflation and worn tread lengthen stopping distance.
NARRATION: Check tread and pressure regularly — underinflation and worn tread lengthen stopping distance.

SLIDE 3 | Lights and signals
Confirm brake, turn, and headlamps work. Test headlights, brake lights, and turn signals so others can predict your moves.
NARRATION: Test headlights, brake lights, and turn signals so others can predict your moves.

SLIDE 4 | Mirrors and seat
Adjust before you shift into drive. Set seat, mirrors, and steering wheel before you move — not while rolling.
NARRATION: Set seat, mirrors, and steering wheel before you move — not while rolling.

SLIDE 5 | Windshield and wipers
See clearly in rain. Keep glass clean and replace streaking wipers before the storm arrives.
NARRATION: Keep glass clean and replace streaking wipers before the storm arrives.

SLIDE 6 | Fuel and range
Do not run to empty. Keep enough fuel for traffic delays — running out on a freeway is a hazard.
NARRATION: Keep enough fuel for traffic delays — running out on a freeway is a hazard.

SLIDE 7 | Warning light response
Red means stop safely soon. Learn your dash icons — red warnings usually mean stop safely and get help.
NARRATION: Learn your dash icons — red warnings usually mean stop safely and get help.

SLIDE 8 | Brake feel check
Soft pedal means get service. If the brake pedal feels soft or sinks, do not drive — have the system checked.
NARRATION: If the brake pedal feels soft or sinks, do not drive — have the system checked.

SLIDE 9 | Child seat basics
Install correctly; never front airbag with rear-facing. Use the correct car seat for age and size and never place a rear-facing seat in front of an active airbag.
NARRATION: Use the correct car seat for age and size and never place a rear-facing seat in front of an active airbag.

SLIDE 10 | Loose cargo
Secure loads so nothing becomes a projectile. Unsecured cargo can injure occupants in a sudden stop — stash or strap it down.
NARRATION: Unsecured cargo can injure occupants in a sudden stop — stash or strap it down.

FACT: Always verify rules in the current California Driver's Handbook.
