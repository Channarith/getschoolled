LESSON: Lab Safety Fundamentals
LANGUAGE: en
AUDIENCE: corporate
TRACK: Safety
LEVEL: Role-based required
ROLE: Lab and R&D teams
DELIVERY: 50–60 min live class
FIT: Anyone working in wet labs, electronics labs, or shared experimental spaces.
SUMMARY: PPE, chemical hygiene, eyewash/shower use, labeling, and emergency response in laboratory environments.

SLIDE 1 | Lab Safety Mindset
Labs combine chemicals, energy, tools, and people. Most incidents come from
rushing, skipping PPE, or poor housekeeping. Slow down, prepare, and respect
procedures.
NARRATION: Labs combine chemicals, energy, tools, and people.

SLIDE 2 | PPE and Engineering Controls
Wear required eye protection, gloves, and lab coats. Use fume hoods and
ventilation correctly. PPE is the last line of defense, not a substitute for
safe methods.
NARRATION: Wear required eye protection, gloves, and lab coats.

SLIDE 3 | Chemical Hygiene
Read the SDS, label secondary containers, segregate incompatibles, and never
pipette by mouth. Know where spill kits and SDS binders live.
NARRATION: Read the SDS, label secondary containers, segregate incompatibles, and never pipette by mouth.

SLIDE 4 | Emergency Equipment
Locate eyewash stations, safety showers, fire extinguishers, and exits before
you start work. Practice how to use them. Seconds matter after a splash.
NARRATION: Locate eyewash stations, safety showers, fire extinguishers, and exits before you start work.

SLIDE 5 | Housekeeping and Reporting
Keep aisles clear, dispose of waste correctly, and report near misses. A clean
lab is a safer lab.
NARRATION: Keep aisles clear, dispose of waste correctly, and report near misses.

FACT: Labs combine chemicals, energy, tools, and people.
FACT: Wear required eye protection, gloves, and lab coats.
FACT: Read the SDS, label secondary containers, segregate incompatibles, and never pipette by mouth.
FACT: Locate eyewash stations, safety showers, fire extinguishers, and exits before you start work.
FACT: Keep aisles clear, dispose of waste correctly, and report near misses.
