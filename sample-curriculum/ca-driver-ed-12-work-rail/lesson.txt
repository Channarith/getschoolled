DELIVERY: 15–20 min short session

SUMMARY: Cones, flaggers, gates, and tracks. Study aid only — not a DMV-approved driver education course.

SLIDE 1 | Work zone speed
Obey reduced speeds; fines often double. Slow to the posted work-zone speed — workers are close to traffic and fines often increase.
NARRATION: Slow to the posted work-zone speed — workers are close to traffic and fines often increase.

SLIDE 2 | Follow the flagger
Hand signals override your usual plan. Flaggers control temporary right-of-way — stop or proceed exactly as directed.
NARRATION: Flaggers control temporary right-of-way — stop or proceed exactly as directed.

SLIDE 3 | Lane shifts and tapers
Merge early at cones. When cones create a taper, merge as soon as you can do so safely — avoid the last-second dive.
NARRATION: When cones create a taper, merge as soon as you can do so safely — avoid the last-second dive.

SLIDE 4 | Workers on foot
Give a wide berth. Expect workers near the edge of the lane — leave extra lateral space.
NARRATION: Expect workers near the edge of the lane — leave extra lateral space.

SLIDE 5 | Night work zones
Glare and temporary lights confuse depth. Night construction lighting can hide cones — slow more than you think you need to.
NARRATION: Night construction lighting can hide cones — slow more than you think you need to.

SLIDE 6 | Railroad flashing lights
Never drive around gates. When lights flash or gates lower, stop at least 15 feet from the nearest rail.
NARRATION: When lights flash or gates lower, stop at least 15 feet from the nearest rail.

SLIDE 7 | Multiple tracks
Wait for a second train. After one train passes, look both ways — another train may come on a second track.
NARRATION: After one train passes, look both ways — another train may come on a second track.

SLIDE 8 | Stalled on tracks
Exit immediately; run at an angle toward the train. If your vehicle stalls on tracks, get everyone out and move away at an angle toward the oncoming train.
NARRATION: If your vehicle stalls on tracks, get everyone out and move away at an angle toward the oncoming train.

SLIDE 9 | Quiet zones
Trains may not horn — still stop for signals. In quiet zones trains may not sound horns — signals and gates still mean stop.
NARRATION: In quiet zones trains may not sound horns — signals and gates still mean stop.

SLIDE 10 | Emergency vehicles at tracks
Never follow EMS onto tracks. Do not follow an emergency vehicle across tracks unless signals are clear for you too.
NARRATION: Do not follow an emergency vehicle across tracks unless signals are clear for you too.
