Accreditation and full-program completion
=========================================

A lesson is one sitting. The harvest cap stays about 12 slides, with a hard
ceiling of 24. A full course is the program: at least 200 slides, partitioned
into those sittings. A deck of 20 slides or fewer is a sample or a single
lesson. It is not program completion.

What exists today
-----------------
CERTIFIABLE_LESSONS (aoep_shared.learnable.index) marks lessons that require
a registered account to start and a verified pass token to be marked PASSED.
Guests can still take sample lessons. That gate is necessary and it is not
accreditation.

Most of those lessons are 5 to 30 slides. Naming a body on a short deck
(AHA, ASE, OSHA, CA DMV, CompTIA) means the lesson is aimed at that body's
topics. It does not mean Salareen issues that body's card.

What a completion record requires
---------------------------------
A Salareen program completion record is written only when all of these hold:

1. The published program has at least 200 slides.
2. The program includes a summative check, not only teaching slides.
3. A human of record has signed the published program. Generated drafts
   stay unsigned (human_reviewed is false).
4. The learner has a registered account.
5. A verified summative pass token is present. The learner cannot self-award.

may_award_program_completion in aoep_shared.full_programs enforces that
order. The lesson API reports the first gap as completion_block. It does
not set program_completion_eligible from an anonymous status call.

Outside cards are not issued
----------------------------
request_external_card always returns external_card_not_issued.
external_card_issued is false on every manifest and status payload.

- CPR / first aid: posture external_prep, body AHA. A Salareen record, once
  the rules above are met, says the learner finished this training program.
  It is not an AHA or Red Cross card. The skills test for that card belongs
  to that body.
- Automotive: posture external_prep, body ASE. Same split. ASE certification
  is ASE's.
- AI, grade school, and vocabulary: posture salareen_completion. The record
  is a Salareen learning record. Grade school completion is not a state
  diploma. Vocabulary completion is not a language diploma.
- Driver education, food handler, workplace, and other catalog families are
  discovered by scripts/expand_full_programs.py. The same rule applies:
  short families are expanded into review drafts; families that already
  publish 200 or more slides are left in place; no outside card is issued.

Pipeline
--------
python3 scripts/expand_full_programs.py

Default domains: ai, cpr, automotive, grade-school, vocabulary.
--all includes every other sample-curriculum family.
--plan-only writes program.json without lesson files.
The full run writes lesson-sized course JSON and pptx under
output/harvest/programs/<program_id>/lesson-NN/.

Those files are review drafts (status review_draft). Publishing them into
the live catalog, and signing human_reviewed, is a later editorial step.
Until that happens, completion_block stays below_program_slide_floor or
awaiting_human_review, and no completion record is awarded.
