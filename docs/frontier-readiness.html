Subject dependency — the spread is the bias that matters

Protected attributes are kept out of the training context by the data guardrail. That is the right ban, and it is not a substitute for a subject floor. A learner in linear algebra is not getting the same tutor as a learner in geometry. For an accredited course that difference is a dependency: the credential is only as strong as the weakest scored objective.

Truth curve

The code that stands in for this curve is check_grounding. It splits an answer into claims and scores token overlap with the lesson passage. A claim needs support of at least 0.50. The answer passes when at least 70% of claims clear that bar. Otherwise the tutor is supposed to abstain and quote the lesson.

That guard is the right shape for education. It is not yet a calibration curve. Token overlap can mark a reworded true sentence as unsupported, and it can mark a fluent false sentence as supported if the words match. Frontier use needs three extra marks on every scored answer: grounded or not, abstained or not, and whether a human later agreed. Plot those. The teal diagonal is the target. The live tutor has not been plotted.

Deep specializations and tabular mastery

The product already names specialist prep: OSHA, HIPAA, food handler, CompTIA, HVAC EPA, ASE, pharmacy tech, real estate, CPR, DMV. Those ids sit in CERTIFIABLE_LESSONS. A specialization is reliable only when each objective has its own row: items, difficulty, pass mark, and a human-signed key. That is a table, not a chat model.

We do not have a fitted tabular model (no item-response, no logistic mastery, no per-objective standard error). What we have is the subject table above, 20 tags, one overlap mean each, on 41 items total. A frontier education system keeps the language model for explanation and a small tabular model for “can this person be marked passed on this objective.” The table is what an auditor can recompute. The language model is not.

Accreditation dependencies

A completion record is withheld until three things are true together: the published program has at least 200 slides, it includes a summative check, and a human of record has signed it. The view returned to the app sets the award to none and the external card to false. Guests can sample. A credited attempt needs a registered account, and a face, voice, and government-id check is required before the first full credited lesson. Membership tier is a separate gate. None of that issues an OSHA card, a DMV card, or a state diploma. Prep courses can teach toward those bodies. Salareen does not print their cards.

Scenarios

Demo, Intro to Machine Learning

A visitor taps Start. The lesson is ten English parts: examples, splits, features, the training loop, overfitting, evaluation, deployment, ethics, then a recap. Mandarin and Khmer text for the eight teaching parts is already written and is the best AI lesson we have. The app will not play it, because the stored files are 108 segments. Cantonese has no text, so HiuMaan has nothing in Cantonese to read. This is the course to keep as the demo. It is not ready to demonstrate three languages.

Khmer parent, audio on the drive

They pick Khmer. The gateway that was translating is down. The cached lesson is the wrong shape, so the voice speaks English with a Khmer voice setting only if the client forces it, and the words will not match. Sreymom can speak real Khmer once a 10-segment file exists. The fine-tune is not what they will hear.

Cantonese learner

There is no phrase course, no NLLB code, and no yue.json. Ingest directories exist for training a voice later. A demo that promises Cantonese today would be reading Mandarin with a Hong Kong voice, which is the wrong language.

Accredited OSHA pass

The id is listed, with a 2.0 CEU label and the body name OSHA. A guest is stopped. A registered learner still cannot receive a completion record or an outside card. The module says so on purpose. Signing that program on top of a 31% overlap tutor would put a name on a score that has not moved in 40 cycles.

Where to go next, in order

Stop treating a repeated promotion as progress. Point the Spark eval at the frozen 200-item set and keep the adapter only when that number rises. Add a subject floor so linear algebra cannot hide inside a 31% average. Log groundedness, abstention, and later human agreement so the truth curve becomes a plot instead of a drawing. Restart translation only for the 222-course catalog, at concurrency 1 on this machine, and do not load a second Khmer run from the base weights while that eval needs the GPU. Write Cantonese as its own language or keep it out of the demo. Leave external cards off until a person of record signs a program that clears the subject floor.
