Truth curve

Logged rows now carry groundedness and abstention. Mean groundedness is 23.3% on 14800 rows. Abstention accuracy is 50.7%. Human agreement is — from 0 reviews.

The code that stands in for the curve is check_grounding. A claim needs support of at least 0.50. The answer passes when at least 70% of claims clear that bar. Otherwise the tutor is supposed to abstain and quote the lesson. Token overlap can mark a reworded true sentence as unsupported, and a fluent false sentence as supported when the words match. The missing mark is whether a person later agreed.

Deep specializations and tabular mastery

The product already names specialist prep: OSHA, HIPAA, food handler, CompTIA, HVAC EPA, ASE, pharmacy tech, real estate, CPR, DMV. Those ids sit in CERTIFIABLE_LESSONS. A specialization is reliable only when each objective has its own row: items, difficulty, pass mark, and a human-signed key. That is a table, not a chat model.

There is still no fitted item-response or logistic mastery model. What we have is the subject table above, plus a frozen 200-item exam and a 96-item STEM exam. The language model explains. The table is what an auditor can recompute.

Accreditation dependencies

A completion record is withheld until the published program has at least 200 slides, it includes a summative check, and a human of record has signed it. The training floor is 40%. The accreditation floor is 70%. The view returned to the app sets the external card to false. Guests can sample. A credited attempt needs a registered account, and a face, voice, and government-id check is required before the first full credited lesson. None of that issues an OSHA card, a DMV card, or a state diploma. Prep courses can teach toward those bodies. Salareen does not print their cards.
