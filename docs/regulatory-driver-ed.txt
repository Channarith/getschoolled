REGULATORY FLAG: SELLING A "CALIFORNIA DRIVER EDUCATION" COURSE ONLINE
======================================================================
Written 2026-10-25. NOT LEGAL ADVICE. The owner should confirm all of this with counsel and with the DMV before launch.

TOP OWNER DECISION
  Decide, with counsel, whether the paid Driver's Education course is sold as:
    (A) general learning and practice, clearly NOT a DMV-approved or DMV-licensed driver education course; or
    (B) a course that is meant to satisfy California's driver education requirement for a license, which (see below) appears to
        require a DMV-licensed driving school and DMV-issued completion certificates.
  Until the owner confirms a licence, the product copy must say (A). Everything shipped in this work assumes (A).

WHAT THE DMV SAYS (found by search on 2026-10-25; read the pages yourself, they change)
  1. California DMV, "Driving School Operator License":
     https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-industry-services/occupational-licensing/occupational-licenses/driving-school/driving-school-operator-license/
     A driving school needs a licensed owner, operator and instructor to give instruction. The page lists qualifications
     (minimum age 21, background clearance, a written exam, and, for internet-only schools, "60 hours of driving school operator
     training"). The page does not say in so many words that an unlicensed provider may not sell driver education; it describes
     the licence as the way to operate a driving school. COUNSEL SHOULD CONFIRM what an unlicensed seller of "driver education"
     may and may not say or do.
  2. California DMV, "Driver Training Schools": https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/driver-training-schools/
  3. California DMV, "Online Learning": https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/online-learning-and-tests/
  4. California DMV, "Instruction and Learner's Permits": https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/dmv/detail/teenweb/permit_btn1/driver_ed
     From the search summaries: driver education may be 30 hours or 2 1/2 semester periods of classroom instruction, or a home
     study or internet training program, and an internet program "must meet the same requirements as classroom instruction".
  5. Certificates of completion. Search results describe DL 400C as the Certificate of Completion of Non-Classroom Driver
     Education issued by DMV-licensed driving schools, and say each school is accountable for the certificates (DL 400B, 400C,
     400D, OL 800) issued to it. A seller that is not a licensed school should not issue anything that looks like one.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PRODUCT (assumption A)
  * The receipt, the checkout card and the Terms of Sale say: "AI-assisted instruction. This course is not a DMV-approved or
    DMV-licensed driver education course, and completing it does not satisfy California licensing requirements, unless the
    course page says otherwise." The wording is configuration (COURSE_DMV_DISCLAIMER): counsel should set the final text.
  * No completion certificate that could be mistaken for a DMV certificate may be issued without the owner's confirmation.
  * The refund policy blocks automatic refunds after "a certificate is issued": decide what a Salareen certificate is and is not.
  * Other rules may apply (consumer protection, education or vocational-school law, minors' contracts, auto-renewal rules,
    payment-processor restrictions on regulated education). They were not researched here.

COPY AUDIT (searched apps/web, the course studio, shared lessons, the mobile app)
  * No product text claims DMV approval or that the course satisfies licensing. The searches covered "DMV-approved",
    "approved by the DMV", "DMV-licensed", "satisfies ... requirement", "certificate of completion", "DL 400".
  * To check: subrepos/theodore_course_studio/src/theodore_course_studio/cert_multimodal.py line 271 says "Short lessons plus
    official CA DMV practice tests". If those are the DMV's own practice tests linked from dmv.ca.gov this is fine; if they are
    our own practice tests, the word "official" could mislead. Not changed here.
  * lesson_visuals.py tells learners to check each state's official DMV handbook: good, keep.

OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE OWNER / COUNSEL
  1. Does selling this as test prep (not as the required driver education) need any DMV licence? What may the marketing say?
  2. If the aim is (B): licensing path, approved curriculum, who issues certificates, bonding and record-keeping duties.
  3. Is a "Driver's Education" title itself a problem under (A)? Consider "California permit test prep".
  4. Apple App Review and Google Play will look at the same claims: keep the app text consistent with (A).
