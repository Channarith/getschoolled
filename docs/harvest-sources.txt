HARVEST SOURCES AND LICENSES
============================
Plain text on purpose (repo no-markdown house rule). Retrieved 2026-10-24.

NOT LEGAL ADVICE. This is a technical summary of what each source's own pages say.
Share-alike, attribution wording, derivative-course handling, and whether AI generation
counts as an adaptation all need the owner's legal review before anything ships.

WHY THIS EXISTS
  Salareen sells subscriptions, so everything in the product catalog must be usable
  commercially and adaptable into courses. The harvester used to accept cc-by-nc,
  cc-by-nc-sa and "oer" and trusted whatever license string the seed supplied. Several
  seeds were also wrong (OpenStax was listed as CC BY; it is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The code is
  now aoep_shared/harvest/licensing.py (tiers, parsing, page checks), vetted_sources.py
  (registry and do-not-harvest list) and provenance.py (credit and evidence per course).

TIERS
  COMMERCIAL_OK   CC0, public domain, CC BY (any version, with attribution), MIT, Apache-2.0,
                  BSD, US federal government works. Ingested.
  SHARE_ALIKE     CC BY-SA, GFDL. Commercial use is allowed with attribution AND the derivative
                  course must be offered under the same license. Ingested only when
                  harvest.allow_share_alike is ON (default OFF; env AOEP_HARVEST_ALLOW_SHARE_ALIKE=1
                  or harvester --allow-share-alike). Courses carry license_obligations, an
                  attribution line, a "Sources and licenses" slide, and ATTRIBUTION.txt.
  NON_COMMERCIAL  any NC variant. Rejected.
  NO_DERIVATIVES  ND without NC. Rejected (we adapt content into courses).
  PROPRIETARY     all rights reserved, paywalled, login-walled. Rejected.
  UNKNOWN         empty, unparseable, "oer", bare "creative commons". Rejected.
  Rejected pages may optionally go to a separate research-only corpus (harvest.research_only,
  default OFF) that is never published, never searched by tutoring and never exported.

HOW A PAGE IS ACCEPTED
  1. Pre-fetch: host not on the do-not-harvest list; a declared license that can never be used
     is refused before any request.
  2. Fetch: HARVEST_USER_AGENT must name a real contact (the crawler refuses to run without
     one); robots.txt is read, cached for 24 hours and honored including Crawl-delay (a robots.txt
     server error blocks the host for an hour; a 404 means no restrictions); per-host minimum
     delay (HARVEST_HOST_DELAY, default 2 s) on top of the global rate cap; every redirect hop is
     re-checked; 401/402/403 and login/paywall URLs are refused; 20 MB cap.
  3. Page check: the page's own license is read from link rel=license, meta license/DC.rights tags,
     schema.org itemprop=license, JSON-LD "license", and footer text. Conflicting evidence rejects
     the page. A seed's license is only a claim: it must agree with the page.
     A page with no license markup is accepted only when the host is a vetted site below, or the
     seed carries license_verified_by + license_verified_at + license_evidence.
     noai/noindex (meta robots or X-Robots-Tag), paywall markers and JSON-LD isAccessibleForFree=false
     reject the page.
  4. Provenance is stored in the corpus DB, in the course JSON, and in ATTRIBUTION.txt.

PER-SOURCE FINDINGS (primary source, retrieved 2026-10-24)

  NOT USABLE COMMERCIALLY (do-not-harvest list)
  OpenStax        CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. "Request permission for commercial use" through OpenStax's
                  Content Reuse form. Formerly CC BY; the old seed (cc-by) was wrong.
                  https://help.openstax.org/s/article/Licensing-information-of-OpenStax-textbooks
                  https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/preface (footer + preface)
  MIT OCW         CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. "You may not use the material for commercial purposes."
                  https://ocw.mit.edu/pages/privacy-and-terms-of-use/
  Khan Academy    CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for materials; Terms of Service forbid scraping and using content to
                  train or fine-tune AI models. (Found through search result summaries of the support
                  pages; the TOS page itself did not render, so re-check before relying on exact wording.)
                  https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/42929097425037-What-s-allowed-and-not-allowed-under-Khan-Academy-s-Terms-of-Service
  CK-12           Historically CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; now a "CK-12 Curriculum Materials License" under its Terms of
                  Use. Not commercial. (Search summary only; the licensing page returned 404.)
                  https://info.ck12.org/terms-of-use
  Coursera, Udemy, edX, Skillshare, LinkedIn Learning, Quizlet, Chegg, Course Hero
                  Proprietary platform or login-walled content. Terms of service not read for this
                  document: UNVERIFIED, treated as not usable. Coursera pages made up 183 of the 271
                  sources in the local corpus (see the audit).
  YouTube, Vimeo  Captions and videos are not openly licensed course text. Do not harvest as course text.
  arXiv           The overwhelming majority of papers use arXiv's non-exclusive license, which grants no
                  reuse rights; only papers whose abs page shows a CC license could qualify. The old seed
                  (cc-by for all of arXiv) was wrong.
                  https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/reuse.html
  Stanford Engineering Everywhere, composingprograms.com and similar university course seeds
                  Declared CC BY-NC-SA / CC BY-NC in the old seed file. Moved to seeds/research-only/.

  VETTED, USABLE WITH CONDITIONS
  Wikipedia, Wikibooks, Wikiversity (text)
                  CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL (dual). SHARE_ALIKE tier: attribution (hyperlink or URL to the
                  article, or a list of authors) and share-alike. Images and files carry their own
                  licenses: text only.
                  https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use (section 7)
  Project Gutenberg
                  "The vast majority of Project Gutenberg eBooks are in the public domain in the US."
                  Commercial use of the Project Gutenberg name/trademark needs a royalty, so strip the
                  Gutenberg boilerplate. Not all items are public domain outside the US.
                  https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html
  NASA            "NASA content ... generally are not subject to copyright in the United States." Items
                  marked as third-party are copyrighted; the NASA insignia is not public domain.
                  https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
  US federal agencies (nih.gov, medlineplus.gov, eeoc.gov, dol.gov, osha.gov, census.gov)
                  17 U.S.C. 105: no copyright protection for works of the US Government, but the
                  government may hold copyrights transferred to it.
                  https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html
                  MedlinePlus: health topic summaries, tests and genetics pages are public domain; the
                  A.D.A.M. encyclopedia (/ency/) and drug monographs (/druginfo/) are copyrighted and are
                  excluded in code. Acknowledge the source ("Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine").
                  https://medlineplus.gov/about/using/usingcontent/

  PER-ITEM: NOT VETTED SITE-WIDE (the page itself must show the license)
  LibreTexts      Each page carries its own license: CC BY, BY-NC, BY-NC-SA and others; remixed pages can
                  be "undeclared". https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/Remixer_University/LibreTexts_Construction_Guide_1e/03:_Basic_Editing/3.09:_Selecting_Copyright_for_Page_(Meta-Tags)
  OER Commons     Resources carry their own Creative Commons licenses, including NonCommercial and
                  NoDerivatives; the site's own pages are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. (Search summary; the site
                  returned 403 to automated fetch.) https://oercommons.org/
  Open Textbook Library (open.umn.edu)
                  Per-book licenses: CC BY, BY-SA, BY-NC, BY-NC-SA, GFDL; ND is excluded by the library.
                  https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/faq
  Saylor Academy  Saylor-authored content is CC BY 3.0 Unported; third-party resources inside courses
                  carry their own (often non-commercial) licenses. https://www.saylor.org/OpenContent
  Internet Archive
                  Each item has its own license (licenseurl or rights metadata); most texts have no license
                  tag at all, so "public domain" is never assumed.
                  https://archive.org/post/1056247/how-do-i-see-the-licence-of-a-specific-file
                  (search summaries only; the Internet Archive Terms of Use were not read directly).

  UNVERIFIED (could not be read; treated as needing the page to carry a license)
  data.gov        Federal datasets are not all public domain; many carry other licenses. The help page did
                  not load. Seed license removed.
  loc.gov         Library of Congress rights vary by collection. Not researched. Seed license removed.

WHAT CHANGED FOR EXISTING DATA
  scripts/harvest_license_audit.py reads a corpus DB read-only, sorts every source into ok /
  needs_review / quarantine, and with --apply marks quarantine candidates quarantined (reversible with
  --restore; nothing is deleted). Quarantined sources drop out of corpus search and out of RAG
  retrieval. Run it on every machine that crawls:
    python3 scripts/harvest_license_audit.py                      # read-only report
    python3 scripts/harvest_license_audit.py --apply               # quarantine
    python3 scripts/harvest_license_audit.py --restore             # undo
  Local audit of output/harvest/harvest_corpus.db on 2026-10-24: 271 sources, 10,522 courses,
  132,924 chunks. quarantine: 216 sources, 9,309 courses, 121,650 chunks. needs_review: 28 sources
  (data.gov, loc.gov), 555 courses. ok: 27 sources, 658 courses.

SERVING
  Nothing from the harvester is posted to the production catalog today (output/ is git-ignored and no
  service reads it). aoep_shared.harvest.serving.course_servable must be called by any future publish
  step. scripts/present_course.py refuses courses whose provenance says the license is not usable
  (override --allow-unlicensed for private review only) and warns on courses with no provenance.

OUTSIDE THE HARVESTER (needs a decision)
  aoep_shared/drive_topic_harvest.py builds Drive Mode audio courses from Wikipedia text (CC BY-SA 4.0
  and GFDL) with an LLM and serves them. It records no attribution or license. Wikimedia's terms require
  attribution and share-alike for adaptations. This was not changed here because it is a product and legal
  decision (credit line in the course, same-license offer, or replacing the source).
