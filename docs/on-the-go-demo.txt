On the Go demo: courses, avatar, face presence, always-on questions
====================================================================
Page: /demo/on-the-go (hidden: four quick clicks on the logo). Component: apps/web/app/components/OnTheGo.tsx (mode "demo").

What the learner gets
  - Course search (always visible at the top, both demo and signed-in): fuzzy matching (typos, accents, CJK/Thai/Khmer substrings) over title,
    translated title, category, subject and tags; title, length, a "Resume at part N" mark; arrow keys + Enter; windowed list so the whole catalog stays fast.
    The VOICE decides the language: courses with a shippable translation in the voice's language come first with a green "Ready in <language>" badge;
    others say "English only" (or the languages they are ready in). "Include English-only" (remembered) hides them. No translated courses yet for the
    language -> a short note and the English courses. Say "search for ...", "find me a course on ...", "clear search".
    Demo searches the 16 curated courses (apps/web/app/lib/onTheGoCatalog.ts DEMO_COURSE_IDS); flag on_the_go.demo_full_search (default OFF) opens the whole
    catalog to the anonymous demo. Signed-in users search the full catalog. Flag on_the_go.demo_course_picker (default on) turns the demo picker off.
  - Which translations are listed: GET /audio/search (services/curriculum, packages/shared aoep_shared.audio_search) lists a language for a course only when the
    library file has the same segments as today's English, is a translation of today's English (provenance sha) and still passes the v2 validator
    (translation_v2.is_current). Held, stale, partial or wrongly filed files are never listed. Narration is live text-to-speech in the picked voice
    (audio_mode = live_tts; there are no per-course pre-rendered clips). The server library folder is AOEP_COURSE_TRANSLATION_DIR; an empty folder means every
    course is English only.
  - Resume: the segment each course was left at is stored on the device (localStorage salareen.onTheGo.progress.v1, last 60 courses).
  - Voice commands (after "Hey Sala", or just spoken while a lesson plays): "play the Roman Empire course", "next course",
    "previous course", "play another course", "what courses are there". Playback commands (pause, faster, repeat) are unchanged.
  - Sala avatar, bottom left, on screen in EVERY state (loading, before a course, playing, ended, error; a link to sign in on the signed-out player):
    gold ring pulse + green mic dot + "Listening: ask me anything" while the microphone is on; dimmed with a slashed mic and "Muted. Tap to listen again"
    when muted; "Tap Sala, then ask me anything" before the first Start. Tap to ask; speaking at any time interrupts the lesson, the answer is spoken, the lesson resumes.
    data-sala-avatar marks it for the shared giggle/dance module.
  - One-time consent card before the first microphone prompt (Turn on microphone / Microphone and camera / Not now). It says plainly that the browser's speech
    recognition turns the voice into text and some browsers send that audio to their provider; the camera is optional, on this device only, never uploaded or
    recorded. The choice is remembered (salareen.onTheGo.consent.v1); "Privacy choices" reopens it. Nothing is opened before the card is answered.
  - Top-right widget (movable, position remembered): camera preview with a status dot, mic mute, camera on/off.
    Face away for 4 s pauses the lesson; back for 0.7 s resumes it. A pause the learner made themselves is never undone by it.
  - Always-on questions: while a lesson plays the browser recognizer listens (wake word, or a real question that is not the lesson's own audio).
    The lesson pauses, the answer is spoken in the voice picked in the account menu (same-gender fallback for custom voices), then the lesson resumes.
    Demo answers come from the course text itself (no paid model call). Signed-in users keep the Sala voice agent.

Privacy and limits
  - The camera opens only after the learner taps Start, and only if it is not switched off (salareen.onTheGo.camera = off is remembered).
    Frames go to the browser's own face detector (MediaPipe Face Landmarker from /vendor/vision, else the Shape Detection API). Nothing is uploaded or stored.
  - A hidden tab, a locked screen or a phone call stops the camera and the recognizer; both come back when the page is visible again.
  - Question limiter: 6 questions a minute and 40 a session (friendly spoken message). Course and playback commands do not count.
  - Browser limits: the Web Speech recognizer needs Chrome/Edge/Safari and a secure context; Firefox has none (the mic button is disabled, lessons still play).
    Android WebView and iOS WKWebView stop microphone and camera when the app is in the background or the screen is off; narration may continue
    only where the OS allows background audio. Use headphones: the recognizer cannot subtract the lesson's own sound (a text echo filter catches most of it).
  - Not verified on real phones: needs the Camera check screen / device test checklist.

LAYOUT (v0.97.86): one dropdown, one widget
  Course list: a closed dropdown that shows the current course. Opening it shows about six rows with a type-to-filter box on top
  (still the pre-translated search: ranked by the voice's language, "Ready in <language>" badges, include-English toggle). The
  anonymous demo offers 6 curated courses (DEMO_COURSE_IDS in app/lib/onTheGoCatalog.ts); flag on_the_go.demo_full_search opens the
  whole catalog; signed-in users keep the full catalog. A spoken "search for ..." opens the list with that query.
  Widget: ONE movable card, top right (OnTheGoPresence.tsx). It holds the golden Sala head (tap = ask or listen, long-press = mute or
  unmute, data-sala-avatar for giggle and dance), the camera preview with a status dot, the demo timer (anonymous demo only), a
  minimise button and a three-dot menu. The menu has Microphone on/off, Camera on/off, the narrator voice, Full screen, Privacy
  choices and the headphones tip. There are no standalone mic, camera, fullscreen or avatar buttons on the page. Full (signed-in)
  mode keeps a small ? help button in the widget bar because the tutorial promises it; the demo's ? is the tutorial's own.
  End of the demo: the lesson, microphone and camera stop and a card says "Create or log in to your account" (Create account, Log in,
  Not now -> the landing page). No price and no payment: nobody without an account can pay.

DANCE EASTER EGG IN THE WIDGET, AND THE NATIVE SPEECH BRIDGE
  The Sala head in the widget carries data-sala-avatar and the shared module (sala_interactions.js, PR #838) attaches to it: a quick
  side-to-side scrub (mouse hover, or a finger down on Sala) makes her dance; she also dances while the lesson plays and while the
  microphone is muted. The module sets touch-action:none on the head only, so a finger scrub does not scroll or zoom the page.
  Dragging the widget starts ONLY from the grip (the dotted handle and the empty part of the bar), never from the Sala head or the
  camera preview, so a scrub over Sala cannot move the widget. A long-press on Sala mutes the microphone, but the timer is cancelled
  as soon as the pointer moves more than 10 px, so a slow scrub does not mute.
  The module is re-attached after the consent choice (the widget is re-keyed then).
  Spoken "dance" requests ("please dance", "can you dance for me", "shake shake", ...) come from the browser's SpeechRecognition.
  iOS WKWebView has none and Android WebView is inconsistent: where speech recognition is unsupported the scrub keeps working and the
  page shows no extra text (Sala's state is exposed as a tooltip and to screen readers only).
  Native bridge (no native module is implemented here): an app can pass recognised text to the page with
      window.__salaNativeSpeech.onTranscript("please dance")
  (inject it with the WebView's evaluateJavaScript / injectJavaScript). The text goes through the same handler as the browser recognizer:
  dance requests, "hey sala ..." course commands and questions. It is ignored before the demo can drive or after the demo has ended,
  and capped at 300 characters. Reduced motion gets the gentle bob (the module's own CSS).
