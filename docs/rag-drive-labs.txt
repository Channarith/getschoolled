Small-model answer eval (offline harness, no lab POST, no telemetry)
  scripts/rag_small_evals.py scores generated answers on the original 16
  golden items and on the extended set (those 16 plus golden_heldout.jsonl).
  Default models: Qwen2.5 0.5B / 1.5B / 3B Instruct, the STEM QLoRA adapter,
  and the llama-server reference. GPU work takes ~/.gs-gpu.lock.
  Another machine's test port:
    make rag-small-evals RAG_EVAL_BACKEND=http RAG_EVAL_HOST=127.0.0.1 RAG_EVAL_PORT=18042
  Reports land in output/spark/rag-small-evals/ (gitignored).
  The recommendation is the smallest model within RAG_EVAL_MARGIN (default
  0.05) of the reference on answer F1 and on groundedness, scored separately
  for the original 16 items and for the extended set. A higher token F1 does
  not offset answers that are less grounded in the retrieved passages.
