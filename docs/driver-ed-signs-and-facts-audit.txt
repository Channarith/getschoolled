DRIVER ED: SIGN ART AND FACT AUDIT (2026-10-25)
===============================================

Trigger: the owner saw the "School Bus Stop Ahead" sign drawn pale green, with dark-green text spilling outside the shape,
and asked whether it should be red. It should not: no school sign is red. Red is for STOP, YIELD, DO NOT ENTER, WRONG WAY and
prohibitions. School signs are yellow-green or yellow with a BLACK legend and border.

Review gallery (all 140 signs): docs/driver-ed-sign-gallery.html (regenerate: python -m theodore_course_studio.traffic_signs OUT.html)
Before/after of the slide: docs/driver-ed-signs/school-bus-slide-before.png and ...-after.png
Tests: subrepos/theodore_course_studio/tests/test_traffic_sign_art.py (colour/shape rules, centring, no-overflow for all 140 signs,
golden SVGs for 14 key signs: UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 regenerates them after a reviewed change).

SOURCES (what was actually read)
  S1  MUTCD 2003 Edition rev 1, Part 7B (FHWA): s7B.07 "School warning signs shall have a yellow background with a black legend and
      border unless otherwise stated", fluorescent yellow-green (FYG) allowed for S1-1 and S4-5; s7B.10 School Bus Stop Ahead (S3-1)
      placement. https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/htm/2003r1/part7/part7b.htm  (later editions renumber S3-1 as s7B.13 and allow FYG for it)
  S2  MUTCD R1-3P ALL WAY plaque: white legend and border on a red background, s2B.07 (Figure 2B-1). https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/htm/2009/part2/fig2b_01_longdesc.htm
  S3  CA DMV Driver Handbook, Laws and Rules of the Road, traffic signs: stop = red octagon; yield = red triangle; warning = diamond;
      school zone = "5-sided" sign; school crossings may have yellow crosswalk lines.
      https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/laws-and-rules-of-the-road/
  S4  CA DMV, Teen drivers (instruction permit, provisional licence): permit applicants "at least 15 1/2 years old but under 18", parent
      signature, vision exam, knowledge test; hold the permit "at least six months", "50 hours" of practice "including 10 hours of night
      driving", supervising driver "25 years old or older". https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/teen-drivers/
  S5  CA Vehicle Code 22454 / DMV School Buses section: stop for a school bus with flashing red lights and stop arm from either direction on
      an undivided road; no need to stop for a bus on the other side of a divided highway or a road with two or more lanes in each direction;
      amber lights mean slow down and prepare to stop. (read via search summaries of the DMV commercial handbook s10 and the statute text)
  S6  CA Vehicle Code 42010 (work-zone fine doubling): applies in marked safety-enhancement or construction zones with workers present.
  S7  MUTCD S5-1 School Speed Limit: vertical rectangle, FYG SCHOOL panel above a white speed-limit panel (retailer and MoDOT EPG summaries).

 10  Lane-control signs      five faces all read just "ONLY". NOW: arrows (straight, left, right, left+straight, right+straight, left+right).
