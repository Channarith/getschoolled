THE DANCE EASTER EGG (not in the tutorial; the tutorial only says "Sala has a few secrets...")
  scrub:  quick side-to-side motion across Sala: >= 4 swings (3 reversals) in 1.2 s, each >= 40% of her width. Mouse hover or a finger
          down on her (touch-action: none on the avatar only). Not triggered by a tap, a slow drag, small jitter, scrolling, or dragging
          the widget handle. The click that ends a finger scrub is swallowed so it does not toggle the mic.
  voice:  short imperatives only: "dance", "please dance", "can you dance for me", "do a dance", "shake shake", "shake it", "show me your
          moves", "boogie", "let's dance", "get down", "bust a move", with fuzzy matches ("dans", "daance"). Checked BEFORE the question path:
          no paid call. A question about dancing ("what is the history of dance") still goes to Q&A. Other UI languages: es fr de it pt vi zh
          yue ja ko th km (MACHINE-DRAFTED: a native speaker should review); English always works.
  dance:  ~4.8 s sway/bounce/spin with sparkles on the golden Sala cutout; reduced motion = gentle bob; 10 s cooldown; any tap/click or a
          "sala:attention" event stops it. Sound (soft arpeggio) only when narration is not playing, the mic is not listening and sound is
          on; On the Go is visual only (its mic is always on). No line of speech yet: a pre-rendered cheerful clip can be hooked in `onDance`.
  menu:   "Make Sala dance" entry exists behind the flag localStorage aoep.flag.sala_dance_menu = "1" (default OFF).
  where:  studio demo/full course/clean course (any #theodore-avatar-wrap or [data-sala-avatar] element, now or added later), On the Go
          (the Sala button), and anything else carrying data-sala-avatar. Source: voice_static/sala_interactions.js (one module; On the Go
          imports it from /api/studio/voice-overlay/). NOTE for the Driver's Ed flow work: give the new Sala element data-sala-avatar.
