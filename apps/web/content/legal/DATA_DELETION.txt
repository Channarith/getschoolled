SALAREEN — DATA DELETION REQUEST
================================
Version: 1.0   Effective: 2026-07-23
URL: https://www.salareen.com/data-deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Salareen personal data
(account, learning records, optional biometrics, and related operational data),
consistent with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, COPPA, FERPA (via your institution), and similar
laws.

1. WHO CAN REQUEST DELETION
   - An individual account holder (or parent/guardian for a child account)
   - An authorized school/institution administrator for student records under
     their control (school-official / DPA relationship)

2. HOW TO SUBMIT A REQUEST
   Option A — Email (preferred for audit trail):
     To: privacy@salareen.com
     Subject: Data deletion request
     Include: the email on your Salareen account, full name, and whether you want
     (i) full account deletion or (ii) specific data categories only.

   Option B — In-app / account:
     Sign in → Account → contact Support, or use Contact → Support with subject
     "Data deletion request".

   Option C — Institutional:
     School admins should email privacy@salareen.com with institution name and
     the student identifiers under their directory.

3. WHAT WE DELETE
   When a verified deletion request is approved we delete or anonymize, as
   applicable:
   - Account profile and authentication credentials
   - Learning history, assessments, homework submissions, and mastery signals
   - Rewards/points tied to the account (where not legally required to retain)
   - Optional biometric embeddings and consent records for vision features
   - Device/session tokens associated with the account

4. WHAT WE MAY RETAIN (LIMITED)
   We may retain narrowly scoped records when required by law or legitimate
   dispute/fraud prevention (e.g. billing invoices, security logs) for the
   minimum period required, then delete or anonymize them.

5. TIMELINE
   We acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete verified deletions within
   30 days (or sooner where local law requires), unless a lawful extension
   applies. Complex institutional requests may take longer; we will explain why.

6. VERIFICATION
   To protect against unauthorized deletion we may ask you to verify control of
   the account email or provide institution authorization before acting.
