SALAREEN - Terms of Sale
==========================

DRAFT FOR REVIEW. This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer and may change before it takes effect.

1. Who sells.
The seller is the legal entity named on your receipt. Our contact details are on every receipt and on the Contact page.

2. What you are buying.
A one-time purchase gives your account access to one course (for example, the full Driver's Education course). The price is shown before you pay. Access belongs to your account, not to a device: if you buy on the web you can use the course in our apps, and if you bought in an app you can use it on the web, as long as you sign in to the same account.

3. Before you can buy.
You need an account and you need to be signed in. We do not take payment from visitors who are not signed in. The free 10-minute demo needs no payment. Administrators and members on a plan that includes courses (VIP or Premium) do not pay for courses.

4. Price, tax and payment.
The price is set by us and shown at checkout in US dollars. If sales tax applies, it appears as its own line before you pay and on your receipt. Card payments are handled by our payment processor and PayPal payments by PayPal; your card number never reaches our servers. Purchases made inside the iPhone or Android app are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms, and they are the seller of record for the payment.

5. Receipts.
We create a receipt for each purchase. You can open, print or save it as a PDF from your account, and you can ask us to email it. A receipt shows only the last four digits of a card.

6. Refunds.
See the Refund Policy. In short: a web purchase can be refunded during a short window if little of the course has been used and no completion certificate has been issued. Purchases made through Apple or Google are refunded by them.

7. What the course is, and is not.
Courses are AI-assisted instruction. They are for learning and practice. Unless a course page clearly says otherwise, a course is not a DMV-approved or DMV-licensed driver education course, and completing it does not satisfy California licensing requirements or any other legal requirement. Always check the official source (for example, the California DMV) for current rules. We do not promise any particular result on an exam or test.

8. Your licence to use the course.
You may use the course for your own learning. You may not copy, resell, share your account, or record and redistribute the lessons.

9. Changes and availability.
We may improve, update or retire a course. If we retire a course you have paid for before you finish it, we will tell you and, if we cannot give you a reasonable alternative, refund you.

10. Chargebacks.
If you dispute a payment with your bank or PayPal, access to the course ends while the dispute is open. Please contact us first; we want to fix problems.

11. Accounts and deletion.
If you delete your account your access ends. We keep purchase records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.

12. Young learners.
Learners under the age of majority should buy with a parent or guardian. [Wording for counsel.]
